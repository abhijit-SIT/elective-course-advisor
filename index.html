Key Evaluation Directives:

  • Zero Backend: Runs directly in the browser with no paid keys or logins.
  • Top 3 Combinations: Recommends the best 3 elective combinations for selected student.
  • Hard Constraint Adherence: Strict timetable slot clash avoidance, exact quota verification, and prerequisite satisfaction.
  • Trade-off Transparency: Explicitly states what the student gives up (sacrificed courses, extra hours, difficulty risk).
  • Unmet Needs Reporting: Diagnoses when requirements cannot be met.
  • Universal Testing: Tested against every student in the dataset.
